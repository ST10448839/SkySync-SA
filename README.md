# ☀️ SkySync SA

### **Simple weather. Smarter planning. 🇿🇦**

SkySync SA is a modern Android weather application designed to provide **fast, simple and understandable local weather information** for South African users.

Instead of overwhelming users with excessive information and complicated screens, SkySync follows a **"forecast first"** approach. The most important information — such as the current temperature, weather condition, rain probability, wind and feels-like temperature — is displayed immediately, while additional information is available when needed.

## 🌤️ What SkySync Offers

* 🌡️ **Current Weather** — Temperature, conditions, feels-like temperature, wind, humidity and rain probability.
* 🕐 **Hourly Forecasts** — View upcoming weather conditions throughout the day.
* 📅 **7-Day Forecast** — Plan ahead with a simple extended forecast.
* 📍 **Saved Locations** — Search, save, rename and manage favourite South African locations.
* ☔ **Plan My Day** — Provides simple weather-based advice, such as when to carry an umbrella.
* 🚨 **Weather Alerts** — Optional notifications for severe weather, upcoming rain and daily forecasts.
* 🗺️ **Radar** — An optional radar/map experience without making it the focus of the home screen.
* 📡 **Offline Access** — Previously downloaded forecasts remain available when there is no internet connection.
* 🔄 **Automatic Sync** — Changes and updated forecasts synchronise when connectivity returns.
* 🌍 **Multiple Languages** — English, Afrikaans and isiZulu support.
* 🌙 **Customisation** — Dark mode, temperature units, wind units, notification preferences and data-saver settings.

## 🇿🇦 Built for South African Users

The **"SA"** in SkySync SA represents its focus on South African users. The application allows users to search for South African towns and provides language support for **English, Afrikaans and isiZulu**.

## ✨ Designed Around Simplicity

SkySync was inspired by research into existing weather applications including **AccuWeather, Weather: Forecast & Radar Maps, and Weather & Radar Forecast**.

The research showed that these applications offer powerful features such as detailed forecasts, alerts and interactive radar, but can also contain large amounts of information and additional features. SkySync takes useful ideas from these applications while focusing on a smaller, clearer experience.

> ### **See the weather you need. Plan your day. Keep moving. ☀️**

## 🛠️ Technology

SkySync SA is planned as a **Kotlin Android application** using:

**Android Studio** • **Firebase** • **Cloud Firestore** • **Firebase Cloud Messaging** • **Room** • **WorkManager** • **Node.js / Express** • **Open-Meteo API** • **Render**

The application combines cloud services with local storage to provide secure accounts, personalised settings, weather information, notifications and offline functionality.
